This update brings a handy new feature along with a key fix. 

New: 🌐 Incoming Mail Translation - Canary can now translate incoming emails, so you can read messages in any language right in your inbox.
Fixed: 🔄 Zoho Account Sync - Resolved an issue where Zoho account disable status failed to sync across devices when Cross Device Sync was enabled.
Fixed: 📧 Reply "To" Field - Fixed an issue where the "To" field would auto-fill with your own email instead of the sender's when replying to messages received via certain contact forms.

Thanks for flying with Canary - small updates, big difference.
