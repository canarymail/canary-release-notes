This update brings a set of fixes to make everyday email smoother. 📱

Fixed: 🔝 Inbox Scroll to Top - Tapping the Inbox icon at the bottom left now scrolls the inbox back to the top again.
Fixed: 🚀 iPadOS Launch - Resolved an issue where the app got stuck at the splash screen when another window was playing video.
Fixed: ↩ Reply to Sent Emails - Replying to a sent email now correctly fills the “To” field with the recipient instead of your own address.
Fixed: 📶 Sending on Mobile Data - Improved reliability of sending emails over mobile data, including for T-Online.de accounts.

Thanks for flying with Canary - small updates, big difference.
